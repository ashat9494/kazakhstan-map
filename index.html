About Kazakhstan's Map

Kazakhstan's Map (previously MAP KAZAKHSTAN) is an English-language travel atlas and educational project about twelve places in Kazakhstan. Learn and Quiz support English study alongside destination exploration. Geographic boundaries and relief in the Atlas view come from Natural Earth. Some map points are provisional; each card explains its marker and links to its source.

Photograph authors, licences and changes appear in each card. Places without verified images use labelled placeholders.

The original Project map is unavailable here because its publication rights have not been verified. The Baiterek photograph is also omitted pending separate confirmation of rights to the monument as the main subject of the image.
